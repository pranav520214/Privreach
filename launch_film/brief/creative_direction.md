# PRIVREACH 5-MINUTE LAUNCH FILM — CREATIVE DIRECTION

## Campaign Concept
**RESEARCH, CONNECTED.**

## Supporting Brand Line
**Your Private Research Operating Environment.**

---

## 1. Vision & Aesthetic Pillars
- **Minimalist White-Space Product Launch**: Clean, architectural, disciplined, restrained.
- **Editorial Precision**: Generous margins, confident typography, microscopic grid alignment.
- **Scientific Workstation Fidelity**: No neon sci-fi clichés, no generic AI sparkles, no spinning holograms.
- **The Dot Matrix Language**: A distinct 100×100 computational dot matrix representing numerical simulation, wave propagation, and deterministic calculation.
- **Honest Truthfulness**: Real desktop product architecture and verified execution (Gemma 3 local GGUF inference, SymPy/NumPy equation solving, PyMuPDF citation extraction) clearly distinguished from high-level conceptual vision animations.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) and Warm Architectural White (`#FAFAF8`)
- **Typography & Structural Contrast**: Ink Navy (`#172033`), Midnight Charcoal (`#0F172A`)
- **Accent & Continuity Line**: Electric Blue (`#245CFF`, `rgba(36, 92, 255, 1.0)`)
- **Subtle Surface & Borders**: Soft Slate (`#DDE5F2`), Border Mist (`#E2E8F0`), Pale Blue Wash (`#EEF4FF`)
- **Verification Green**: Mint Green (`#10B981`) used strictly for deterministic mathematical proof badges

---

## 3. Typography
- Primary Sans-Serif: **Inter** / Clean Modern Geometric Sans
- Hierarchy:
  - Master Display: 54–72 pt, SemiBold, negative tracking (-0.02em)
  - Section Headlines: 32–40 pt, Medium
  - Mathematical & Technical Annotations: 16–22 pt, Mono / Precise Sans
  - Micro-telemetry: 11–14 pt, Medium, high contrast Navy on Soft Slate

---

## 4. Motion Architecture & Sound Design
- **Camera Movement**: Subtle 2.5D push-ins (1.00 → 1.05 scale over 8–10 seconds), gentle easing (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
- **Line Transitions**: Delicate 1.5px vectors tracing connections between disparate cards, papers, equations, and graphs.
- **Audio Profile**:
  - Narration: Studio-grade documentary voice (calm, intelligent, measured at 125 words/min).
  - Score: Ambient electronic pulses, sparse piano chords with long decay, delicate warm sub-bass pad.
  - Sound Design: Subtle mechanical paper rustles, microscopic ceramic UI clicks, soft harmonic resonance on logo resolves. Zero artificial error beeps or exaggerated sci-fi noises.
